1//! Okumura LZSS decoder (`N=4096`, `F=18`, `THRESHOLD=2`).
2//!
3//! Port of the four near-identical decoder routines in `GAME.unpacked.exe`:
4//!
5//! | file offset | stride | store | trailing byte |
6//! |---|---|---|---|
7//! | `0x2EBEF` (MCG/TGA/CGA) | `0x140` | `stosb` | not consumed |
8//! | `0x2EC8C` (MCG/TGA/CGA) | `0x140` | `xor [es:di],al` | not consumed |
9//! | `0x2FC5D` (EGA) | `0x28` | `stosb` | consumed (`lodsb` at `0x2FCF7`) |
10//! | `0x2FCF9` (EGA) | `0x28` | `xor [es:di],al` | consumed (`lodsb` at `0x2FD9A`) |
11//!
12//! All variants share the format: a 4 KB ring buffer (zero-initialised,
13//! write cursor `r = 0xFEE`), flag bits consumed LSB-first with a `0x100`
14//! sentinel reload, and a terminator of match flag + `00 00` token followed
15//! by a `00` byte.
